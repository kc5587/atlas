Method and caveats

Pressure is descriptive, not a shortage probability or trading signal. Missing data lowers confidence and is not treated as zero. “Supply tightness” is a net-generation headroom proxy, not a formal reserve margin. Execution friction is evidence-only and is not included in the regional score.

Evidence key: observed is directly reported; estimated is scenario or model-based; inferred is an Atlas analytical interpretation.
